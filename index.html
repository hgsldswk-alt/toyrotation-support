Help

  1. Tap Snap a bin and take one photo of each bin of toys.
  2. The home screen shows the bin that's out and how many days are left. Tap Swap to bring out the bin that has been away longest, or tap any bin and choose Bring this one out.
  3. After a swap, tap how it went (loved it, so-so, ignored). Each bin shows its history and how often it was loved.
  4. The gear icon sets the swap schedule, rooms and reminders, and lets you copy or paste a setup code. Add the widget to swap from your Home Screen.
  5. The first 30 days include everything. After that, one room, up to 6 bins and reminders stay free; a one-time purchase unlocks the rest. Use Restore purchase on a new phone.

使用帮助

  1. 点「拍一箱」，每箱玩具拍一张照。
  2. 首页显示现在拿出来的是哪箱、还剩几天。点「换」拿出放得最久的那箱，或点任意一箱选「拿出来」。
  3. 换完点一下孩子的反应（很喜欢/一般/没碰），每箱都能看历史和喜欢比例。
  4. 右上角齿轮可设置换箱周期、房间、提醒，复制或粘贴设置口令。添加小组件可在桌面直接换。
  5. 前 30 天全功能；之后 1 个房间、最多 6 箱和提醒永久免费，一次买断解锁其余功能。换手机用「恢复购买」。

Privacy / 隐私 / 隱私

Toy Rotation does not collect any data. Photos and bins are stored only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or servers.

玩具轮换不收集任何数据。照片和箱子记录只保存在你的 iPhone 上，没有账号、统计、广告或服务器。

玩具輪換不收集任何資料。照片和箱子紀錄只保存在你的 iPhone 上，沒有帳號、統計、廣告或伺服器。
